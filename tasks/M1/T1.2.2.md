## Math

Inline math: the softmax of $z$ is $\sigma(z)_i = e^{z_i} / \sum_j e^{z_j}$.

Display math:

$$
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V
$$
