## Goal
Create the Persian home page, mirroring the English one. The bio is a **faithful Persian translation** of `content-inputs/bio.en.md`. The Owner edits it afterwards (T2.2.3).

## Inputs
- `config/owner.yml`
- `content-inputs/bio.en.md`
- `en/index.qmd` (for reference)

## Files you may create or change
- `fa/index.qmd`

## Steps
1. Replace **all** of `fa/index.qmd` with the content below. Apply the same rules as T2.2.1: fill in placeholders, and delete optional link items whose value is empty.
2. Replace `BIO_FA_HERE` with your Persian translation of `content-inputs/bio.en.md`:
   - Translate faithfully. **Add no facts**, remove nothing, keep the paragraph structure.
   - Keep company, university, product and library names in English, wrapped like `[PyTorch]{.ltr}`.
   - Follow the Persian rules in AGENT_RULES §5.
~~~markdown
---
title: "{{NAME_FA}}"
subtitle: "{{TAGLINE_FA}}"
image: assets/images/profile.jpg
image-alt: "عکس {{NAME_FA}}"
toc: false
about:
  template: trestles
  image-shape: round
  image-width: 14em
  id: about-block
  links:
    - icon: github
      text: "گیت‌هاب"
      href: "https://github.com/{{GITHUB_USERNAME}}"
    - icon: linkedin
      text: "لینکدین"
      href: "{{LINKEDIN_URL}}"

      text: "گوگل اسکالر"

  id: latest-posts

  sort: "date desc"

  fields: [date, title, description]
---
~~~
