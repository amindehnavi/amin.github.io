# T3.5.1 — Series page and series front matter

| | |
|---|---|
| Milestone › Checkpoint | M3 Blog engine › C3.5 Series |
| Who | 🤖 AGENT — level **basic** |
| Depends on | C3.4 approved |
| Time | ~15 min |

## Goal
Add a "Series" page in each language that lists the posts of a series in order. A post joins a series through two front-matter fields:
```yaml
series: "transformers-from-scratch"
series-order: 1
```
To test the page, the temporary hello-world posts get `series-order: 0` in that series. Drafts are hidden, so they disappear at launch.

## Files you may create or change
- `en/series.qmd`, `fa/series.qmd`
- `en/posts/2026-10-hello-world/index.qmd`, `fa/posts/2026-10-hello-world/index.qmd` (front matter only)
- `en/_quarto.yml`, `fa/_quarto.yml` (navbar only)

## Steps
1. `en/series.qmd`, **exact content**:
~~~markdown
---
title: "Series"
toc: false
listing:
  - id: transformers-from-scratch
    contents: posts
    include:
      series: "transformers-from-scratch"
    sort: "series-order"
    type: table
    fields: [series-order, title, date]
    field-display-names:
      series-order: "#"
    filter-ui: false
    sort-ui: false
page-layout: full
title-block-banner: false
---
~~~
