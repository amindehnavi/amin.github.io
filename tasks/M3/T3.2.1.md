## Goal
Create the two templates every new post starts from. The `__TOKENS__` are filled in by `new_post.py` (T3.2.2). They are **not** our `{{PLACEHOLDERS}}`, so leave them exactly as written.

## Background (read only)
- `format: html + ipynb` makes Quarto also produce a downloadable Jupyter notebook of each post. It's linked automatically under "Other formats".
- Both language versions start as `draft: true`.

## Files you may create or change
- `templates/post/en.qmd`
- `templates/post/fa.qmd`
